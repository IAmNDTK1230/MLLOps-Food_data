# Foody RAG chunking decision

The accepted source has 32,457 distinct reviews and a matching Chroma index. One review remains one document; no reviews or restaurants are concatenated. The MiniLM content-token maximum is 2,124; 11,235 reviews (34.6%) exceed the selected FastEmbed model's documented 128-token truncation limit. 222 reviews exceed 512 content tokens. The seven policies in `chunking_comparison.csv` are simulated with the exact local MiniLM tokenizer, splitting only within a review. They are not rebuilt indexes or measured retrieval improvements.

The one-review policy preserves a direct review_id-to-vector correspondence, citation granularity, restaurant filters, and the accepted 32,457-ID integrity check. Splitting long reviews would require chunk IDs, inherited metadata, aggregation back to review IDs, and new retrieval relevance evaluation. This task does not modify the accepted index. The 128-token truncation is a material limitation: tail content of long reviews may not influence their embeddings even though the full review text remains available to BM25 and answer evidence. The one-review policy is the verified demo choice, not proven optimal; a separate chunked-index retrieval evaluation would be needed before changing it.

Actual initial embedding compute time was 1054.63 s at 30.78 documents/s on CPU ONNX. Alternate-policy times in `embedding_workload.csv` are proportional estimates using that observed throughput; they exclude index updates and any retrieval-quality changes. Token counts exclude special tokens and do not apply model input truncation.
