# Failure taxonomy

`failure_counts.csv` contains all saved baseline confusion directions (887 test rows each), all existing Qwen V1 pattern subsets, and all retained V3 rejected-attempt categories. Pattern subsets overlap, so their counts must not be added. Mixed/negation/implicit-word flags are lexical proxies, not human phenomenon labels. The accepted TF-IDF model has 169 test errors; its largest directions are positive→negative (68) and negative→positive (47). Neutral has 43 test rows; 24 are misclassified by TF-IDF. Qwen V1 has 237 errors; 16/24 rows whose text has conflicting gold aspect labels are errors. RAG V3 rejected attempts include topic mismatch and unsupported clauses; final accepted Qwen 3B answers have zero validator-detected unsupported claims in the saved six-case set. The generator gate also correctly refused three insufficient-evidence cases. The small set and automated validator cannot establish a population hallucination rate.
